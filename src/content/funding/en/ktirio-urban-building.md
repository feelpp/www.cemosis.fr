**Ktirio Urban Building** is developed at Cemosis for energy simulation at building and city scales. It combines geometric reconstruction of urban environments, physical modelling and high-performance computing.

The work is supported by [CoE HiDALGO2](../hidalgo2/) and [Exa-MA](../exama/). The urban models are also directly linked to the [FLOOD project](../flood/) for flood simulation.
