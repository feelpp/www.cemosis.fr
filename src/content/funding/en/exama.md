Exa-MA is a targeted project of the NumPEx research programme, funded through France 2030. It develops numerical methods, algorithms and software libraries for exascale architectures.

The University of Strasbourg contributes to project coordination, inverse problems and data assimilation, demonstrators and performance evaluation. Feel++ contributes to this open software environment.
