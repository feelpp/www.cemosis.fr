This CIFRE partnership brings together **AVNIR Energy** and **Cemosis / IRMA, University of Strasbourg**, for the PhD of **Hanna Chetouane**, a former CSMI master’s student, supervised by **Christophe Prud’homme**.

## Supervision

- **Scientific supervision:** Christophe Prud’homme — University of Strasbourg.
- **Scientific co-supervision:** Vincent Chabannes — Cemosis / IRMA, University of Strasbourg.
- **Industry supervision:** Gabriel Georges — AVNIR Energy.
- **Industry co-supervision:** Pascal Vouagner — AVNIR Energy.

## Research abstract

The PhD studies high-order finite-element methods for simulating the transient vibroacoustic response of thin structures. It investigates vibration and wave propagation and coupling between a structure and its surrounding fluid.

The goal is to balance the fidelity of simulated signals with computing costs, contributing to structural health monitoring in industry. Numerical developments will use Feel++ and will be compared with experimental measurements.
