## The challenge

Data4Urbanism studies transport modes in the Strasbourg region. Its goal is to combine mobility data, weather data and air-quality data in a single visualization.

## Objectives

- Process data from available RSS feeds.
- Filter noisy data.
- Observe mobility across the urban area.

## Tools and context

Development primarily uses Python. The work described on the historical site formed part of Xinyue Yang’s final-year CSMI master’s internship, with PEPS AMIES and CNRS funding.
