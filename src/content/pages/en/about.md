## About the platform

Established in January 2013, Cemosis brings together expertise in modelling, simulation, optimisation and scientific computing.

The platform contributes to interdisciplinary projects and collaborations with academic and industrial partners. Its scientific software includes the open-source Feel++ library.

Cemosis is part of [CoRTecS](https://cortecs.unistra.fr/les-plateformes/toutes-les-plateformes), the University of Strasbourg network of scientific research and service platforms.
