On 21 October 2019, Christophe Prud’homme presented Feel++ at the University of Strasbourg Open Access Week.

The event introduced open-source scientific software used in collaborations between Cemosis, other disciplines and businesses.

Open science encourages sharing research publications, data and tools. Accessible software supports this approach by allowing methods to be reused and computations to be better understood.
