## Planned programme

A first draft programme is available: progress reviews of work packages **WP1 to WP7**, PhD and postdoctoral presentations, posters, round-table discussions, intra-work-package meetings and a scientific board meeting.

## Exa-MA and Cemosis

Exa-MA is a targeted project of the NumPEx research programme, funded through France 2030. The University of Strasbourg contributes to project coordination, inverse problems and data assimilation, as well as demonstrators and performance evaluation. Feel++ contributes to this open software environment.
