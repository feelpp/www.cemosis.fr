**Pierre-Antoine Senger** started his PhD on **6 January 2026** at Cemosis / IRMA, University of Strasbourg, within the ANR-funded **JNL-G** project, supervised by **Christophe Prud’homme**.

A graduate of the University of Strasbourg’s **CSMI master’s programme**, he contributes to developing the digital twin of the French National High Magnetic Field Laboratory in Grenoble.

## Research topic

His work focuses on multiphysics modelling and model order reduction: combining detailed finite-element models with reduced-order models that require less computation to simulate the installations. It contributes to JNL-G’s work on magnets, their power supply and cooling systems, using **Feel++** and the **HiFiMagnet** environment.

The project aims to improve facility design and operation, predictive maintenance and energy resource management.
